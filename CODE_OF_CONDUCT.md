# Code of conduct

Azvela seeks a community in which people can collaborate without harassment, intimidation or discrimination. Disagreement and rigorous technical criticism are welcome when directed at the work rather than the person.

## Expected behavior

Act respectfully; listen to different perspectives; give specific, constructive feedback; acknowledge mistakes; protect others' privacy; and credit contributions accurately. Make room for people with different experience, abilities, identities and backgrounds.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory attacks, sexual harassment, doxxing, impersonation, deliberate disruption and retaliation against reporters are not acceptable. Publishing another person's private information without permission, including in technical evidence, is prohibited.

## Scope

This applies to participation in Azvela-managed public project spaces and interactions when representing those projects. It does not replace platform terms or applicable law.

## Reporting and enforcement

Request a private moderation contact via the [contact-only form](https://github.com/Azvelahq/.github/issues/new?template=contact_request.yml) if no project-specific private contact is published. Do not describe incidents or name affected people publicly. GitHub's own reporting mechanisms are also available for platform abuse.

Authorized maintainers should assess reports discreetly, avoid conflicts of interest and apply proportionate measures, which may include warnings, removal of content, temporary restrictions or exclusion. Affected participants may request reconsideration through an appropriate private channel. Confidentiality is limited by investigation needs, platform capabilities and applicable law.

This is Azvela's own community standard, not a claim of adopting or certifying against an external code of conduct.
